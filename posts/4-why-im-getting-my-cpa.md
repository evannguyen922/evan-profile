## Why the CPA still matters

The CPA license lets accountants sign audit opinions, and it tells employers and clients that you've mastered accounting, tax and ethics. It's still the profession's strongest credential.

The [U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/business-and-financial/accountants-and-auditors.htm) says job prospects are best for accountants with a professional certification, especially the CPA. That's a strong reason to start planning early.

## The exam looks different now

Since January 2024, the CPA exam has used a "Core + Discipline" model. Everyone takes three core sections (auditing, financial accounting and regulation), then chooses one discipline section.

## The 150-hour question

Most states have required 150 credit hours for a license, which is about a fifth year of school. A growing number of states, including Pennsylvania, now also accept a bachelor's degree plus two years of experience.
